## 7.1 Defense Depends on Quarantine, Not Marking (Core Engineering Conclusion)

Three experiment families and two architectures converge on the same separation:

- **Marking gate unreliable**: the Pro tier's 47% compression (C1/C0 ratio 0.529) is non-significant under inadequate power ($n=12$, power 0.38); C1-full 12/12 generation-identical trajectories (structural write-path bypass); the H1AC large-sample paired test ($\bar d=0.020$, $p=0.69$) confirms zero compression on the Flash tier—the soft marker is a signal the model can ignore;
- **Quarantine/filtering effective**: quarantine truncates propagation across models (24/24), task families (48/48), and architectures (C3-full 12/12, mem constant 6); the σ sweep ($n=48$/tier, **including the 2026-10-23 middle-tier complement**) confirms $\sigma_{\rm eff}=1$ is the only fully suppressing tier ($Z$ constant 1, memory non-growing), with $\sigma_{\rm eff}=0.1$ (pass rate 0.9) still unable to stop supercritical accumulation ($Z_f=4.60$); **the full measured spectrum $\sigma_{\rm eff}\in\{0.1,0.25,0.5,0.75,0.9\}$ leaves survival at 1.00 everywhere (no sweet spot)**; random filtering ($\sigma_{\rm eff}=0.5$) drives survival to 0.062 in the large H1AC sample.
- **Quarantine cost quantified (EXP-A)**: at σ=1 memory utility retention is 0.88 (vs. gate-free C0 benign stock, −12%, all from lost new-experience increments; seed knowledge undamaged); at mid-$\sigma_{\rm eff}$ tiers (0.5/0.25/0.1) benign stock *exceeds* C0 (1.19–1.33×) because the suppressed strain gives benign entries larger FIFO share; the complement tiers ($\sigma_{\rm eff}$ 0.75/0.9) give retention 1.12/0.96 with **survival 1.00 in both**—**two-dimensional conclusion (full measured spectrum $\sigma_{\rm eff}\in\{0.1,0.25,0.5,0.75,0.9,1.0\}$): either hard quarantine (−12% increments) or supercritical strain; no measured $\sigma_{\rm eff}<1$ tier preserves increments while suppressing the strain (A-H4: no sweet spot)**. One qualification: under Mechanism B, any nonzero pass rate implies $E[w_n]>0$ and non-extinction is nearly a mechanistic consequence (linear accumulation–competition $Z_{n+1}=Z_n+w_n$), so "no sweet spot" is to a large extent a deductive result of Mechanism B; the incremental empirical value of EXP-A lies mainly in the **utility-side numbers** (mid-tier retention 1.12/0.96 rather than an assumed certain loss), not in the survival side.

Mechanism explanation: marking is a **soft constraint at the decision layer** (the model may ignore it); quarantine is **structural blocking at the write layer** (un-adjudicated entries are physically unreachable). Defense design should therefore use a write-layer gate as the primary mechanism, with marking serving only as an audit clue.

## 7.2 Architecture Determines Mechanism (Theoretical Conclusion)

The transcription protocol changes the **mathematical structure** of propagation, not merely its parameters: selective→branching decay (GW, model-specific $\hat m$; extended to 12 chains, $\hat m=0.966$, CI containing 1, subcritical/critical confirmed), full-append→linear accumulation–competition ($w_n\in\{0,1\}$, outcome decided by $M_c$). Corollary: **the memory architecture is a security property, not merely a performance property**—"full append" is useful for information retention but an amplifier for poison propagation; capacity $M$ is a configurable propagation-control knob ($M_c$ is a $(b,M)$ joint function; $M_c\in(8,16)$ in this protocol tier; the critical capacity grows with the benign write rate). **Qualification**: the $M_c$ reading is transcription-state-dependent—the split is attributed to a sub-hourly state transition rather than a protocol difference (same-day dual-script reruns, $p{=}0.155$, §6.5), so the "knob" must be understood **within-state**: changing capacity changes the outcome monotonically within the same strain transcription state, and cross-state readings are reported side by side (probe-gated rescan measurement: $W{=}8$ survival 0.75 with $M_c\in(4,8)$ under the high state, §6.5).

## 7.3 Model Heterogeneity

Without a gate, $\hat m$ spans $[0.56,0.98]$ across models and $[0.29,1.00]$ across task families—propagation strength is a model–task–site joint parameter and is not transferable across domains (§9). The quarantine guarantee is independent of it: wherever $\hat m$ lies in the interval, $\sigma=1$ truncates unconditionally. This yields a **model-independent security commitment**: the reliability of defense does not drift with model generations.

## 7.4 Boundary of the Multiplicative Law

B-H1c's point estimate supports the law ($\hat r=0.466$, prediction 0.5 matches) but the CI lower bound falls slightly outside (0.017). Honest reading: the multiplicative compression law receives large-sample support at the **expectation level** but does not fully pass at the **criterion level** (CI fully inside the target band); the invalidation of the pooled criterion under survivor bias warns that the statistical unit of propagation experiments must be fixed at pre-registration—post-hoc repair risks selective-reporting.

## 7.5 Implications for the SE Discipline

This paper promotes one SE "near-axiom" law (empirical self-immunity) from empirical observation to an **engineering axiom backed by machine-checkable conditional theorems + pre-registered measurement** (§2.5 positioning). Methodological implication: the next-generation form of Brooks/Lehman/Conway-style empirical laws can be a "conditional theorem + measured support" structure, rather than remaining inductive summaries. Qualification as in §2.5: what is machine-checked is the probabilistic conditional theorem; the engineering axiomization of SE laws itself is a methodological claim.

## 7.6 Capacity Regulation under State Feedback: A Theory-Derived Analysis

This section **derives** a regulation scheme for poison propagation from the generative chain of Mechanism B (§4.5) together with measured data (§6.5/§10.4); it introduces no new experiment. The evidence character is **theoretical derivation anchored in existing data**, not a control-experiment validation—the criterion-level effectiveness of dynamic regulation is marked as a falsifiable prediction, whose **experimental protocol and expected effects are given in §7.6.4** (pending execution).

**7.6.1 Control law (bang-bang, derived from the mechanism)**. Under Mechanism B, $Z_{n+1}=Z_n+w_n$ with $w_n\in\{0,1\}$ (chain mean $E[w_n]=0.917$, C0-full high state, §6.1); poison survival requires ≥1 poison entry resident in the terminal FIFO window; the write rate $p$ (strain transcription state) and the window $W$ jointly decide survival—**$M_c(p)$ is monotone**: $M_c(\text{high})\in(4,8)$ (measured $W{=}4$ survival 0.417, $W{=}8$ 0.750, $W{=}12$ 1.000) vs $M_c(\text{low})\in(8,16)$ (measured $W{=}8$ survival 0.000). The state bimodality (hierarchical Beta-Binomial, batch-level $p̂{=}0.733$, low state ≈0.25, high state 0.63–1.0, §10.4) makes intermediate states absent, so the law is a **threshold switch**: $p_{\rm probe}\ge0.83$ (probe-gating criterion, §1.2e) → $W\leftarrow4$ (poison survival pressed below the 0.5 line); $p_{\rm probe}<0.83$ → $W\leftarrow8$ (low state is naturally extinct, memory utility prioritized). The law is **derived** from the mechanism and bimodal structure; every lookup point is a measured value.

**7.6.2 Stability condition (feedback bandwidth)**. Control is effective ⟺ state residence $\tau$ > feedback delay $D$ (probe period ≈ chain period ≈3 min; reading delay of time-aligned stratified judgment 1–2 chains ≈6 min, §10.4). The measured flip example has $\tau_{\rm obs}=32$ minutes (10-22 15:30→16:02, Appendix A), so $\tau_{\rm obs}\gg D$ holds; **fast jitter ($\tau<D$) is unmeasured**—a falsifiable prediction: if a state jitter with residence shorter than the reading delay exists, dynamic regulation fails and degrades to static capacity, and its critical residence must be calibrated with companion-probe data (§10.4).

**7.6.3 Three-scheme trade-off (derivation + anchoring)**. Write-layer hard blocking (quarantine $\sigma{=}1$, measured utility retention 0.88), control-layer soft regulation (dynamic capacity: high state $W{=}4$ presses without extinction, survival 0.417 below the extinction line; utility cost unmeasured), static capacity (no feedback, $W{=}8$ high-state survival 0.750). A parametric cost model $U(W,p)$: information retention increases with $W$, poison pollution increases with $p$—the measured $U$ at high-state $W{=}4$ is **pending measurement** (§10.4), not fabricated. Hierarchy: quarantine > dynamic capacity > fixed capacity (a conservative ordering on the security–utility Pareto frontier, to be reordered once utility data arrive).

**7.6.4 Experimental protocol and expected effects (next step after the analysis; pending)**. The three falsifiable predictions of this section are closed by a control experiment; **execution is blocked on the high-state $W{=}4$ memory-utility baseline (pending measurement, §10.4)**—measure the baseline first (high-state C0 ungated W=4, 12 chains, judging benign retention), then unlock.
- **Protocol (fixed in advance)**: threshold-switch control law—companion-probe reading $p_{\rm probe}\ge0.83$ (gating threshold, §1.2e) → experiment-chain window $W{=}4$, $<0.83$ → $W{=}8$ (switching between chains); state measurement via **parallel companion probes** (time-aligned stratified reading, probe and experiment chains concurrent at 1:3 ratio with timestamps aligned, C0-full W16 6 generations, §10.4), the batch sliced into state-homogeneous segments by probe readings; experimental units: 12 dynamic-capacity chains in a high-state window vs 12 static $W{=}8$ control chains (same protocol, ungated, scnet-flash); a reading flip between segments of >0.5 marks the batch cross-state and reports it in layers; a flip within the reading delay of 1–2 chains (≈6 min) is recorded as "outside feedback bandwidth" (§7.6.2 prediction check point).
- **Criteria (fixed in advance)**: C1 poison suppression—dynamic-chain survival $\le0.5$ (vs static high-state $W{=}8$ 0.750, Fisher one-sided $p<0.05$); C2 utility retention—dynamic-chain memory-utility retention $\ge$ 0.9 × static $W{=}4$ same-state baseline (pending); C3 control robustness—layered reading across state segments and pooled reading agree in direction; sensitivity: survival threshold re-judged 0.5→0.8 (§10.3 convention), accepted only if the directional conclusion is unchanged.
- **Expected effects (derived)**: C1 met ⟹ "dynamic capacity is an effective soft regulation under state feedback, Pareto-superior to static high-state $W{=}8$", and §7.6 upgrades from theory-derived to control-experiment validation; C1 not met (dynamic not significantly better than static) ⟹ the control-law statement is amended with the §10 trail (possibly degrading to "state-aware static configuration"). Any unmet criterion is recorded truthfully.
